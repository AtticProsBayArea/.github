## 👋 About us

Attic Pros keeps attics and crawl spaces clean, insulated and rodent-free. Our crews remove old insulation and
contamination, seal entry points, install new insulation, and send before-and-after photos of every job.

- ⭐ **4.8** from **1,900+** customer reviews
- 💎 Diamond Certified
- 🕐 Open 24/7 for scheduling and customer support
- 🪪 CSLB Lic. #1022608 · SPCB Co. Reg. #9901 (Branch 2)
